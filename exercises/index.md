# Exercise Index

Register of every exercise, remediation problem, capstone and interview drill assigned in the apprenticeship. Claude adds a row at assignment and updates the status at grading.

- **exercise_id:** `NN-A` per module; remediation problems `NN-A-R1`; capstones `C1`–`C4`; final assessment `FRA`; interview drills `INT-NN`.
- **Brief:** `lessons/NN_*/exercises/<exercise_id>.md` (capstone specs in `curriculum/capstones/`).
- **Submission:** `submissions/<exercise_id>/`.
- **status:** Assigned · Submitted · Graded – passed · Graded – remediation · Replaced.
