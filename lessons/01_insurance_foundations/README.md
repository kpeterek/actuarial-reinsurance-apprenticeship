## Planned concept sequence

Taught one concept at a time, with a comprehension question after each (see `CLAUDE.md`). The sequence may be compressed or expanded to fit the gradebook when the lesson is generated.

1. The insurer's business model: premium in, losses and expenses out, float invested in between; combined ratio and underwriting profit; leverage
2. The policy contract: exposure, coverage, limit, deductible, premium; occurrence vs aggregate limits
3. Premium accounting: written, earned, unearned, in-force; cancellations, endorsements, audits
4. The claim lifecycle and loss accounting: paid, case, incurred, IBNR, ultimate; ALAE and ULAE
5. The ratios: frequency, severity, pure premium, loss cost, loss ratio, expense ratio, combined and operating ratios
6. Year conventions: accident, policy, calendar, and report year; development age
7. Gross, ceded, net; direct vs assumed
8. Reading an insurer's financial statements: income statement by line, reserves and surplus, prior-year development, Schedule P, statutory vs GAAP
