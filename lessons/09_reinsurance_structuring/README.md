## Planned concept sequence

Taught one concept at a time, with a comprehension question after each (see `CLAUDE.md`). The sequence may be compressed or expanded to fit the gradebook when the lesson is generated.

1. What problem is the program solving? Framing a structuring question
2. Retained vs ceded loss distributions
3. Volatility, tail risk, and earnings stability metrics
4. Capital implications and the cost of reinsurance: margin ceded vs capital relief
5. Comparing retentions and limits: lower, higher, and wider layers
6. Quota share vs excess of loss vs aggregate protection
7. Casualty issues: long vs short tail, social inflation, limits profiles, attachment erosion
8. Claims-made vs occurrence, and why casualty reinsurance is hard to price
9. The structuring recommendation memo
