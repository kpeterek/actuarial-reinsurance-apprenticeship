## Planned concept sequence

Taught one concept at a time, with a comprehension question after each (see `CLAUDE.md`). The sequence may be compressed or expanded to fit the gradebook when the lesson is generated.

1. Why insurers buy reinsurance: capacity, volatility, capital, catastrophe protection
2. The parties and contract forms: cedent, reinsurer, broker; treaty vs facultative
3. Proportional reinsurance: quota share and surplus share; ceding, sliding-scale, and profit commissions
4. Non-proportional reinsurance: per-risk XOL, catastrophe XOL, clash, aggregate excess, stop loss
5. Layer language: retention, attachment, limit, exhaustion, occurrence definitions
6. Reinstatements and reinstatement premium
7. Premium mechanics: subject premium, rate on line, minimum and deposit premium, adjustable premium, brokerage
8. Gross, ceded, and net: applying a program to losses
9. Reading Kettlerock's own reinsurance program

## Exercise theme

Apply Kettlerock's 2025 reinsurance program to a set of occurrences, compute ceded and net results, explain reinstatement premium, and write a one-page program summary for a new CFO.
