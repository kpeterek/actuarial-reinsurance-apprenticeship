## Planned concept sequence

Taught one concept at a time, with a comprehension question after each (see `CLAUDE.md`). The sequence may be compressed or expanded to fit the gradebook when the lesson is generated.

1. Layer losses and the limited-expected-value identity
2. Attachment and exhaustion probabilities
3. Experience rating I: as-if losses, trend, development, on-level subject premium
4. Experience rating II: burning cost, credibility, and large-loss considerations
5. Exposure rating for casualty: increased limits factors and limits profiles
6. Exposure rating for property: exposure curves and policy profiles
7. Reconciling experience and exposure rates
8. Monte Carlo simulation of layer losses
9. From expected loss cost to technical premium: expenses, brokerage, risk margin, cost of capital
10. Technical vs quoted price: why the market differs

## Exercise theme

Price a Kettlerock casualty excess layer by experience and exposure rating, reconcile the two, and propose a quote.
