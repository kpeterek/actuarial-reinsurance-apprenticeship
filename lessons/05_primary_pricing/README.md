## Planned concept sequence

Taught one concept at a time, with a comprehension question after each (see `CLAUDE.md`). The sequence may be compressed or expanded to fit the gradebook when the lesson is generated.

1. The ratemaking equation and the two indication methods: loss ratio and pure premium
2. Premium on-leveling: parallelogram method vs extension of exposures
3. Exposure and premium development: audits and cancellations
4. Loss development for pricing (a preview of Module 06)
5. Trend periods and applying frequency and severity trend
6. Catastrophe and large-loss adjustments
7. Credibility: classical and Bühlmann, and choosing a complement
8. Expenses (fixed vs variable), profit provision, and the permissible loss ratio
9. Segmentation, rate adequacy, and actual vs expected
10. From indication to selection: what an actuary actually recommends

## Exercise theme

Prepare a rate indication for a casualty line with a recommendation.
