## Planned concept sequence

Taught one concept at a time, with a comprehension question after each (see `CLAUDE.md`). The sequence may be compressed or expanded to fit the gradebook when the lesson is generated.

1. How catastrophe models work: hazard, vulnerability, exposure, and financial modules
2. Event catalogs, event loss tables, and year loss tables
3. Average annual loss and its uses
4. OEP vs AEP, exceedance probability, and return periods
5. PML, VaR, and TVaR: what each says and does not say
6. Primary, secondary, and model uncertainty
7. Accumulation, concentration, and diversification
8. Evaluating a catastrophe XOL program from a year loss table, including reinstatements
9. Portfolio dashboards in Power BI
10. Explaining tail metrics to non-actuaries

## Exercise theme

Evaluate the adequacy of the property catastrophe program from the year loss table and explain a 100-year PML to a CFO.
