## Planned concept sequence

Taught one concept at a time, with a comprehension question after each (see `CLAUDE.md`). The sequence may be compressed or expanded to fit the gradebook when the lesson is generated.

1. Exposure bases and why each line uses the one it does
2. Frequency, severity, and pure premium, and how they decompose a change in loss ratio
3. Severity distributions: lognormal, gamma, Pareto; fitting by maximum likelihood and judging fit
4. Limited expected value and capping
5. Large losses: thresholds, excess loss provisions, and stability
6. Measuring trend: frequency and severity separately, exponential fits, choosing the fitting window
7. Segmenting results: class, territory, limit, and state views of the same total
8. Compound Poisson and aggregate loss
9. Working in Python: a reproducible first notebook
