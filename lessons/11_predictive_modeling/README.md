## Planned concept sequence

Taught one concept at a time, with a comprehension question after each (see `CLAUDE.md`). The sequence may be compressed or expanded to fit the gradebook when the lesson is generated.

1. Exploratory data analysis for modeling, on an exposure-weighted basis
2. Sampling, confidence intervals, and hypothesis tests on insurance data
3. Linear regression and why insurance needs GLMs
4. GLM structure: distributions, links, offsets, and weights
5. Frequency models: Poisson and negative binomial
6. Severity and pure premium models: gamma and Tweedie
7. Logistic regression for binary outcomes
8. Validation: train/test, overfitting, variable selection, interactions, residuals
9. Lift, double lift, and calibration
10. Reading and modifying a GLM script in R; when judgment should override the model

## Exercise theme

Build frequency and severity GLMs for a casualty line and compare them with your Module 05 aggregate indication.
