## Planned concept sequence

Taught one concept at a time, with a comprehension question after each (see `CLAUDE.md`). The sequence may be compressed or expanded to fit the gradebook when the lesson is generated.

1. Insurer financial analysis: underwriting vs investment income, ratios, and decomposing premium growth
2. Prior-year development, Schedule P, statutory vs GAAP, and AM Best data
3. Model review: how to review someone else's model
4. Executive communication: the 200-word summary and the five-minute verbal briefing
5. Defending an analysis: underwriter, broker, and chief actuary challenges
6. Automating a recurring exhibit: Power Query and Excel automation revisited
7. Interview drills: technical
8. Interview drills: case and behavioral
9. Packaging portfolio work for employers
