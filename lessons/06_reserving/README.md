## Planned concept sequence

Taught one concept at a time, with a comprehension question after each (see `CLAUDE.md`). The sequence may be compressed or expanded to fit the gradebook when the lesson is generated.

1. Why loss reserves are the largest number on the balance sheet
2. Building triangles from transactions: paid, reported, and claim counts (Excel and pandas)
3. Age-to-age factors, averages, and selecting factors
4. Tail factors and cumulative development factors
5. Chain ladder on paid and reported data
6. Expected loss ratio and Bornhuetter-Ferguson methods
7. Cape Cod (concept)
8. Diagnostics: paid-to-reported ratios, closure rates, average case reserves, calendar-year effects
9. When patterns change: changing claim settlement patterns and other operational changes (Berquist-Sherman concepts)
10. Reserve uncertainty, ranges, sensitivity, and the reserve memo

## Exercise theme

Reserve review of a long-tailed line: build triangles, select factors, compare methods, and reconcile the differences.
